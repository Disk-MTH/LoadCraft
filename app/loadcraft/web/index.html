Filter 0.50

Software smoothing of the sensor reading. Higher is more reactive with less smoothing; lower is smoother but lags behind the pull and the release. At the board's 10 readings/s, 0.50 settles in about a third of a second; the higher the value, the snappier.

Storage

Changes apply immediately but live in RAM only. Without a save they are lost when the board is unplugged — the EEPROM is written on demand only, so it is not worn out by every slider move.
